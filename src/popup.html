✨ Sentence practice (Gemini API)
Used by the ✨ buttons on the review page — both the example-sentence generator and the writing-practice polish tool — to call Gemini with your saved words. Stored locally in this browser; sent only to Google's Gemini API when a sentence is generated or polished. Leave auto-generate off to only call the API when you click a button yourself — it saves the wait on cards you don't need it for. Sentences and writing-practice attempts are saved per word (up to 8 each, oldest non-favorite dropped first) so you can revisit them later, and they're included in Export/Import (Sync) above.
