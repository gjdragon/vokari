Turn new words into a story

Pick a word pool above — this week's new words, last month's, whichever level you like — and hit "＋ New Story". Vokari picks whichever words fit naturally into a short, easy-to-follow story; it won't force every word in, so feel free to generate several short stories from the same pool instead of one long one. Highlighted words show their translation on hover, and you can play the whole thing aloud once it's ready.
